THEM 1947 Alien Greys Collection He's watching. He's always watching. And chances are, he's already seen you. Standing an intimidating 5 FEET TALL, THE OBSERVER is one of the most mysterious members of the THEM 1947 Alien Greys crew. Unlike his fellow Greys, THE OBSERVER isn't interested in getting his hands dirty. His job is simple: WATCH EVERYTHING, REPORT EVERYTHING, AND NEVER BE SEEN. From lurking outside bedroom windows in the middle of the night to silently observing unsuspecting civilians being abducted by THEM spacecraft all around the world, THE OBSERVER has witnessed things that would make even the most hardened conspiracy theorist sleep with the lights on. But his duties don't stop at monitoring humans. THE OBSERVER has also been known to stand quietly in the shadows while fellow THEM crew members deal with those cheap, Temu looking knockoff aliens that have been showing up around the galaxy. Witnesses claim he's watched more than a few of these unfortunate impostors get knocked down the steps they were sitting on before being hauled out and dumped near the cornfields. Apparently, THEM 1947 has very little tolerance for low quality extraterrestrial knockoffs. Some say he can stand motionless for hours. Others claim that if you catch him looking through your bedroom window, it's already too late. And if you happen to spot him standing at the edge of a cornfield late at night, you might want to keep moving. Because THE OBSERVER never travels far from the rest of THEM.
