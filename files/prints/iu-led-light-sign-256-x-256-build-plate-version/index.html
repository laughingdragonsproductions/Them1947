IU LED Light - 256 × 256 Build Plate Version Show your Hoosier pride with this IU LED light, designed for printing on a 256 × 256 mm build plate . It can be displayed on a desk or mounted on a wall. You'll need an LED strip, available through MakerWorld or Amazon. Use a strip that's at least 6.56 feet long . A 3.28-foot strip is too short. You'll need to trim the longer strip to fit the light. To keep the strip from falling inside the light, secure it with hot glue or superglue about every inch. CA glue works very well. Follow the adhesive manufacturer's instructions and take care not to get glue on the visible front of the light. These models are tuned for FDM and resin printing, with tree supports recommended on overhangs such as chin, hands, and feet. Typical settings use 0.16 to 0.20 mm layers, two to three walls, and ten to fifteen percent infill for display pieces. Validated printer profiles include A2L, H2S, H2C, H2D, H2D Pro, H3C. Scale the model for desk collectibles or larger Halloween and UFO diorama props while keeping proportions intact.
