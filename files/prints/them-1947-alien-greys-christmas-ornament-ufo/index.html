The world's population was growing, Christmas wish lists were getting longer, and even Rudolph's shiny red nose couldn't make that sleigh fly any faster. Santa was running out of time. Then, one evening at the North Pole, a strange silver spacecraft appeared over his workshop. Out stepped three little grey aliens with enormous black eyes, four fingers on each hand, and absolutely no idea what a candy cane was. Their leader approached Santa, tilted his oversized head, and spoke. "We've been observing you, Nicholas. Your delivery system is terribly inefficient." Santa crossed his arms. "And I suppose you little fellas think you can do better?" The alien blinked. "We traveled 39 light years to get here. You travel around the entire planet using livestock." Santa couldn't really argue with that. And so began the greatest Christmas partnership in the history of the universe. The aliens installed advanced navigation systems in Santa's sleigh, developed gravity defying cargo storage, and introduced a little something they called time dilation. Suddenly, Santa could visit an entire neighborhood in what appeared to be a fraction of a second. But there was still one problem. Even with all that technology, Santa couldn't possibly visit every home himself. That's when the aliens revealed their biggest secret. They had a fleet. Hundreds of flying saucers, each piloted by a specially trained little Grey, ready to deliver presents to every corner of the planet. Santa was impressed. The reindeer were thrilled. From that night forward, Christmas Eve became a highly classified operation. Santa handles the big deliveries while his alien friends quietly take care of everything else. Those strange lights in the sky on Christmas Eve? Not shooting stars. Not airplanes. And definitely not weather balloons. It's Santa's alien delivery fleet, making sure no child gets forgotten. Of course, the government eventually caught wind of the operation. When mysterious aircraft were spotted flying over New Mexico in 1947, the whole thing was quickly covered up. But here's the part they never told you. Santa insisted that his alien helpers remain a secret. After all, if the whole world knew that flying saucers were delivering Christmas presents, nobody would ever go to sleep on Christmas Eve! And to this very day, the partnership continues.
